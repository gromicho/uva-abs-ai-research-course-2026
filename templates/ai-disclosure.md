| Field | Answer |

| Rules for reviewers (uploading manuscripts to AI tools) | |

Mark anything the policy does not cover as "not specified". Do not fill gaps with what the agent thinks is likely.

Three examples, checked on 9 October 2026, show why there is no single rule:

- **American Economic Review:** AI cannot be an author. AI help with drafting or editing must be briefly described at submission. Authors remain accountable and must fact-check outputs.
- **INFORMS:** generative AI is allowed, but use for drafting, translation, summarising and data analysis must be disclosed in the acknowledgements, naming the tool and the help given. Authors remain responsible, including for checking references.
- **Elsevier:** a separate AI declaration is needed for manuscript preparation (basic spelling and grammar checks excepted). AI used in the research, including research code, belongs in the methods section. Reviewers may not upload submitted manuscripts to AI tools.

Policies change. Always check the live page.
