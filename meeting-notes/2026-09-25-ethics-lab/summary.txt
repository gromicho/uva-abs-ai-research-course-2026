Agreement Summary

Curriculum & Ethics Lab
• Applicative AI Ethics: The course will pivot from general AI safety lectures to practical ethics, specifically the responsibility of those with significant computing resources and the impact of draining nonprofit data (e.g., WikiArt).
• Scraping & Legality: The group agreed to demonstrate both legal API usage (Rijks Museum) and "shaded" scraping practices. A geospatial example—manually extracting Google Maps coordinates vs. using the official API—will be added to illustrate common but technically illegal practices.
• The "Good Citizen" Framework: The importance of robots.txt will be framed as a social convention of the internet, akin to not throwing garbage in the street, rather than just a technical barrier.
• Hands-on Demos: Students will compare Claude-generated scraping code against professional-grade scrapers. Live demonstrations will include prompt injections and instructions found in malicious packages to show how to bypass code barriers.
• Curriculum Flow: The AI disclosure inventory will be treated as a "cliffhanger" awareness topic, setting the stage for later sessions with Marlane.
